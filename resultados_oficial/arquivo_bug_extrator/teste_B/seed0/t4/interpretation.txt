The experimental results suggest that the order of few-shot examples may influence the accuracy of the language model in text classification tasks. However, the current setup lacks sufficient statistical power to draw robust conclusions. For instance, in category "17," the model's answer was incorrect despite having three examples in the prompt, which may indicate that the order of examples affects performance. To obtain statistically significant results, the number of examples per category should be increased, and multiple permutations of the same set of examples should be tested. This will allow for a more comprehensive evaluation of how example order impacts model accuracy, including the calculation of mean accuracy and the performance of a t-test to determine significance.
