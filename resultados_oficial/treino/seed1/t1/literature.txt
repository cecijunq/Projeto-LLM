[2501.07653v2] This paper presents a clinical decision support system (CDSS) for mental health diagnosis that combines large language models (LLMs) with constraint logic programming (CLP). The CDSS translates diagnostic manuals into a logic program using an LLM and then solves it using a CLP engine to provide interpretable and accurate diagnoses. The system allows domain experts to inspect and modify the LLM-generated logic program, ensuring diagnostic accuracy and transparency. The work highlights the potential of LLMs in aiding complex diagnostic tasks, which is relevant to the topic of using language models for differential diagnosis in medical contexts.
