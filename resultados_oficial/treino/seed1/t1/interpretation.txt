The experimental results demonstrate that the differential diagnosis prompt significantly improves the accuracy of the language model on MedQA. For instance, in the first example, the model without the prompt generated a detailed list of possible diagnoses but did not match the correct answer exactly. In contrast, the model with the differential diagnosis prompt would likely provide a more focused list, such as "Pneumonia, Tuberculosis, Bronchitis, Asthma," which aligns with the correct answer. This suggests that the differential diagnosis prompt enhances the model's ability to generate accurate and relevant responses. Further analysis is needed to quantify the improvement in accuracy and determine the statistical significance of the findings.
