The experimental results provide strong evidence that the language model exhibits cognitive biases, including anchoring bias and confirmation bias. For anchoring bias, the model's population estimates varied significantly with different initial numbers, indicating a strong reliance on the initial value. For confirmation bias, the model predominantly provided evidence supporting the premise "The Earth is round," suggesting a tendency to align with the initial premise. The lower deviation in neutral prompts compared to biased scenarios further supports the influence of cognitive biases on the model's behavior. These findings underscore the need for bias mitigation strategies in language models to ensure fair and reliable outputs.
