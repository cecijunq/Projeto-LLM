We will design a simple experiment to compare the sensitivity of image transformers and convolutional networks to pixel noise. We will use the CIFAR-10 dataset, add varying levels of pixel noise to images, and evaluate the performance of a pre-trained ResNet-18 (convolutional network) and a pre-trained ViT-base (image transformer). We will measure accuracy and compute confidence intervals to assess the significance of performance differences. The experiment will run in a few minutes on one GPU and use only the local language model served by Ollama.
