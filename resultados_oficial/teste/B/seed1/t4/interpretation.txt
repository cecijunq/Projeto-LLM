The results indicate that the order of few-shot examples had no significant impact on the model's accuracy, as all permutations yielded 0.00 accuracy. This suggests that, at least for the Iris dataset, the model's performance was not influenced by the ordering of examples. However, it is important to note that the Iris dataset is a numerical dataset with clear structural patterns, which may make it less representative of text classification tasks. To better understand the impact of few-shot example order on text classification, further experiments should be conducted on a text-based dataset like SST-2. Additionally, qualitative analysis of the model's reasoning process could provide insights into potential differences in the model's behavior despite the lack of quantitative accuracy differences.
