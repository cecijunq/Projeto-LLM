[2206.00092v1] FHIST: A Benchmark for Few-shot Classification of Histological Images
This paper introduces a public benchmark named FHIST for few-shot classification of histological images. It addresses the gap in existing benchmarks focused on natural images and highlights the importance of few-shot learning in medical imaging due to the scarcity of labeled data. The FHIST benchmark is designed to facilitate research in histology, where fine-grained tissue classification is crucial. The paper emphasizes the need for diverse and high-quality datasets in few-shot learning scenarios, which is relevant to understanding how the order of few-shot examples may affect model performance in text classification tasks.
[2311.14544v1] Inferring Latent Class Statistics from Text for Robust Visual Few-Shot Learning
This paper presents a novel approach that leverages text-derived statistics to predict the mean and covariance of the visual feature distribution for each class, enhancing the robustness and generalizability of few-shot learning models. The method utilizes text to infer latent class statistics, which enriches the latent space and improves performance in cross-domain scenarios. This approach is particularly relevant to understanding how the order of few-shot examples may affect model performance in text classification tasks, as it explores the interaction between text and visual features in few-shot learning settings.
