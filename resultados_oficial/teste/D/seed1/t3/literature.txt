It consists of 180 datasets generated by prompting image search engines and diffusion models, covering natural images, cartoons, specific colors, or objects that do not naturally appear. CIFAR-10-Warehouse is designed to evaluate how well models generalize to out-of-distribution scenarios, which is relevant to understanding the impact of data augmentation techniques like rotation on model robustness.
[2411.15497v3] This paper proposes AeroGen, a layout-controllable diffusion generative model tailored for remote sensing image object detection (RSIOD). It addresses the scarcity of labeled data in RSIOD datasets by enabling the generation of high-quality images with horizontal and rotated bounding box conditions. While not directly focused on CNN robustness or rotation augmentation, AeroGen's ability to generate rotated images could be relevant to understanding how models generalize to rotated inputs, which is closely related to the research topic.
[2510.21391v1] This paper introduces TerraGen, a unified multi-task layout generation framework for remote sensing data augmentation. It addresses the limitations of current generative data augmentation frameworks, which are task-isolated and ignore geographical information and spatial constraints. TerraGen enables flexible, spatially controllable synthesis of remote sensing imagery for various high-level vision tasks, such as detection and segmentation, by introducing a geographic-spatial layout encoder and a multi-scale injection scheme. This framework is particularly relevant to the research topic as it provides a method for generating augmented data with controlled spatial transformations, which can be used to evaluate the robustness of CNNs to rotation and other spatial transformations.
